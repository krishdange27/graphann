# Research Paper

## Title

Making DiskANN Adaptive: Navigation-Aware Search, Diversity Pruning, and Feedback-Driven Graph Refinement for Graph-Based ANN

## Publication

International Journal of Engineering Research and Technology (IJERT)

Volume 15, Issue 06

June 2026

## Authors

- Abhijeet Kumar
- Krish Dange
- Sai Jagadeesh

## Abstract

This work investigates graph-level optimization strategies for graph-based Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search. The proposed system introduces navigation-aware search, diversity-aware pruning, and adaptive graph refinement techniques to improve search efficiency while maintaining high retrieval quality.
